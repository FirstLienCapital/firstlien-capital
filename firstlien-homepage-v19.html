Non-circumvention

FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

What is FirstLien.ai? +

FirstLien.ai is a technology-driven private real estate lending marketplace operated by DirectLenderCapital.com, a licensed mortgage broker. We connect brokers and borrowers seeking private capital with accredited lenders, institutional funds, family offices, banks, hard money lenders, and trust deed investors. All deals are secured by first-position liens on real property.

Does FirstLien.ai hold funds or take title to property? +

No — never. We are a licensed introducing broker only. All loan proceeds are disbursed by the lender's chosen licensed title company or escrow agent. We take no interest in any property and never receive, hold, or control any funds at any point in the transaction.

What loan types are supported? +

Bridge Loans, DSCR Loans (24-month, 5-year ARM, 30-year fixed), Fix & Flip, Hard Money, Construction and Ground-Up, Cash-Out Refinance, and Conventional loans. DSCR loans qualify on property cash flow — no tax returns required.

Who can become a lender on FirstLien.ai? +

Individual trust deed investors, family offices, private funds, banks, credit unions, hard money lenders, and institutional capital entities. All lenders must be accredited investors as defined by SEC Rule 501 of Regulation D, or represent institutional capital. Access is by manual approval — applications reviewed within 1–2 business days.

What yields can lenders expect? +

Deals on FirstLien.ai typically target yields of 8–14% annually, depending on loan type, LTV, property type, and term. All deals are secured by recorded first-position deeds of trust or mortgages. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Lenders should conduct independent due diligence before funding.

What is the fee for submitting a deal? +

$19.99 per single deal submission or $49/month for unlimited submissions. A success fee of 1 point (1%) of the funded loan amount is owed at closing by the submitting party. There is no fee if the deal does not fund. Submission fees are non-refundable.

How does deal exclusivity work for lenders? +

Premium lender members can claim exclusive review windows on individual deals — locking all other capital out while they conduct their diligence. During the exclusive period, no other lender can access full documents or contact the broker about that deal. The broker can release the lock early if a lender is unresponsive, and deals automatically re-open if a claimed deal does not progress.

Is the platform secure? +

Yes. FirstLien.ai is built with multiple security layers: Content Security Policy headers block cross-site scripting and injection attacks, X-Frame-Options prevent clickjacking, HTTPS encrypts all data in transit, and form inputs are sanitized server-side. All submitted documents are stored in secured cloud infrastructure with access controls. We do not store sensitive financial data on the platform — all transactions flow through licensed third-party title and escrow companies.
