Trust Deed Investing 101

How trust deed
investing works.

A trust deed investment is a loan you fund that's secured by a property. The borrower pays you monthly interest. If they default, you have a legal claim on the property. It's been how private lending has worked for a century — FirstLien.ai just makes it accessible, transparent, and fast.

Step 01

You fund a loan

A real estate investor needs capital to buy, renovate, or refinance a property. You provide all or part of the loan — minimum $50K per deal.

Step 02

Secured by the property

Your name goes on a first-lien deed of trust recorded against the property. If the borrower defaults, you have the legal right to foreclose and recover your capital.

Step 03

Collect monthly interest

Borrower pays you 8–14% annual interest — usually monthly — for the life of the loan. At maturity, you get your principal back plus any remaining interest.

The Math of Compounding

Small rate differences.
Outsized results.

Private lending yields typically run 8–14% — substantially higher than public-market alternatives. Over a decade, that gap compounds into dramatically different outcomes.

  • $100K at 12.99% compounded over 10 years → ~$339K
  • The same $100K at S&P's 10.5% average → ~$271K
  • At a high-yield savings 4.5% → just ~$155K
