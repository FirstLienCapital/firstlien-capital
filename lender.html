Important Information & Legal Disclosures

Read this before you invest.

Trust deed investing carries real risk. We're committed to transparent disclosure so you can make informed decisions. Consult your own legal, tax, and investment advisors before participating in any loan.

Platform Scope & Loan Purpose

FirstLien.ai is an online marketplace connecting accredited and qualified private lenders with real estate sponsors seeking investment and business-purpose real estate loans only.

We do not originate, fund, arrange, broker, or facilitate loans secured by primary residences. All loans facilitated through the platform are non-owner-occupied investment property loans, except owner-occupied commercial property loans (which are not primary residences). No consumer-purpose financing is available through this platform.

Because all loans are business-purpose, the platform and the loans facilitated through it are generally exempt from: TILA/Regulation Z, RESPA, the TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure Rule (TRID), and the Ability-to-Repay (ATR) rule under Dodd-Frank. The platform remains subject to applicable state real estate broker licensing, NMLS requirements, fair housing laws, the FTC MAP Rule, and California Department of Real Estate (DRE) Regulation 2848 governing advertising.
