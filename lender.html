Platform Technology

The technology
behind the deal flow

Private lending has always run on spreadsheets and phone calls. We built the platform that replaces all of that — real-time matching, automated valuation, AI screening, and intelligent routing.

01 — Matching
🧠
Gemini AI Scoring

Every deal submission is scored against your criteria profile across 40+ dimensions — LTV, geography, asset class, loan term, borrower experience, exit strategy, and more. High-match deals surface first.

02 — Valuation
📡
Multi-Source AVM

Automated valuations from multiple independent data sources are pulled the moment a borrower enters their property address. You see verified market data alongside stated values — enabling faster, more confident underwriting decisions.

03 — Screening
🛡️
Deal Quality Filter

Deals where the borrower's stated value diverges significantly from AVM data are flagged and require borrower explanation before reaching lenders. You see clean data — not wishful thinking.

04 — Closing
📋
Automated Doc Engine

State-specific loan document packages generated automatically for AZ, TX, NV, NY, MI, and CA. Attorney-reviewed templates. Faster closings. Less back-and-forth.

05 — Co-Lending
🤝
Fractional Invite System

Built-in co-lending infrastructure. Express interest in a deal, flag it as co-fund candidate, and invite another approved lender to split the position. Each party gets their own note. Tracked within the platform.

06 — Monitoring
📊
Live Deal Dashboard

Track every deal you've expressed interest in. See competing offers, deal status, borrower responses, and closing progress — all from a single dashboard. Full transparency, no black boxes.
