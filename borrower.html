Important Legal Disclosures
Broker Marketplace — Not a Lender

FIRSTLIEN.CAPITAL IS A LICENSED REAL ESTATE MORTGAGE BROKER MARKETPLACE, NOT A LENDER. All loans are originated exclusively by participating third-party private lenders. FirstLien.Capital acts solely as a technology-enabled intermediary. We do not make credit decisions, fund loans, guarantee approval, or hold ownership interest in any loans.


No Commitment to Lend

Submission of a loan request does not constitute a commitment to lend. All requests are subject to underwriting, appraisal, title, and lender credit criteria. Rates and terms subject to change without notice.


Business Purpose Only

ALL LOANS THROUGH FIRSTLIEN.CAPITAL ARE FOR BUSINESS, COMMERCIAL, OR INVESTMENT PURPOSES ONLY. Not for personal, family, or household use. Exempt from TILA, RESPA, and consumer protection statutes.

Broker Compensation

FirstLien.Capital receives compensation in the form of origination and broker fees paid at closing. Fee structure is disclosed in loan documents prior to closing. We may receive compensation from lenders in addition to borrower-paid fees.


Equal Housing Opportunity

FirstLien.Capital and all participating lenders comply with equal credit opportunity and fair lending laws. We do not discriminate on any basis prohibited by law.


Privacy

Information submitted may be shared with participating lenders and service providers as necessary to facilitate your loan request. We do not sell personal information to unaffiliated third parties for marketing purposes.
