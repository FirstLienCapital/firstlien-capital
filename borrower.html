🧠
AI-Powered Matching Engine
Our proprietary algorithm analyzes 40+ data points to match your deal with lenders who are actively deploying capital in your exact asset class and geography. Ranked by probability to close.
📊
Automated Property Valuation
Address entry triggers instant AVM pulls from Zillow, Redfin, and additional data sources. Lenders see averaged estimates alongside your stated value — and we flag material discrepancies before they become problems.
🔒
Lowball Protection System
Set a minimum rate floor when submitting. Lenders below your threshold are flagged. We monitor lender behavior across the network — repeat lowballers are removed. Your deal, your terms.
⚡
AI-Guided Closing Checklist
When you accept a term sheet, our AI generates a custom closing roadmap based on what your lender requires — appraisal, 1003, bank statements, entity docs — with instructions at every step.
