NOT A COMMITMENT TO LEND. Submission of an application through this platform does not constitute a commitment, pre-approval, or guarantee of financing. All terms, rates, and availability are subject to change without notice and are contingent upon satisfactory underwriting, appraisal, title review, and lender approval. Rates shown are representative ranges only and individual loan terms will vary based on creditworthiness, property condition, loan-to-value ratio, market conditions, and lender criteria.

LENDER ANONYMITY. The identity of participating lenders is kept confidential until a term sheet is formally issued. FirstLien Capital reserves the right to withhold lender identification to protect its proprietary lender network. Borrowers may not circumvent the platform to deal directly with lenders identified through FirstLien Capital's network.

BROKER COMPENSATION. FirstLien Capital charges a broker fee of 1.0 point (1% of the funded loan amount) on loans up to $2,000,000, and 0.5 points on loans exceeding $2,000,000, payable solely at closing. No upfront fees are charged. If a loan does not fund, no broker fee is owed. Individual lenders may charge additional origination points, processing fees, and closing costs independent of FirstLien Capital's broker fee.
